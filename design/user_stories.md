$\color{yellow}{\text{Who is the user.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{What do they want to do with the tool.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{What needs and desires do they want for the tool.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{What is their skill level.}}$

User Stories:
1. $\color{yellow}{\text{Kian is an avid cyclist and uses a combination of roads and bike trails to bike to work.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Kian wants to see if there are safer routes he could take to work to avoid collisions and high trafficked areas.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{He wants to see specific locations where crashes occur for other cyclists and also see what times and conditions those collisions typically occur under so he can be better prepared to ride the roads safely for himself and others.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Although he is technically sound, he does not have time to download and analyze data himself, so he is looking for an easy, fast interface to work with.}}$
2. $\color{yellow}{\text{Ellington is a quantitative ecologist researcher.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Ellington wants to investigate broad spatial distributional patterns in animal traffic collisions to determine if and where these issues may be relevant to identify potential hypotheses to motivate research projects.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Ellington wants to use a tool that can create nice visualisations and easily filter through the datasets. Spatial map, scatterplots across axis of location/car/etc. state variables, etc.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Ellington is a data scientist, so he is familiar with working through datasets and analysing code. However, Ellington is not a traffic analyst and is not familiar with data syntax, shorthands, data forms, etc. }}$
3. $\color{yellow}{\text{Nick is a meteorologist.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Nick wants to know where/under what conditions his forecasts will be most impactful.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Nick needs to be able to filter crash location/severity by weather conditions.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{He has moderate technical skills but would prefer to be able to quickly, cleanly interface with the program.}}$
4. $\color{yellow}{\text{Jill is a parent of an elementary schooler, middle schooler, and high schooler in North Seattle.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Jill lives too close to the schools to have bus pickup. They want to see the safest route for their children to walk.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Jill needs to be able to look at data over a long period, over more recent periods, and the trend over time.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Jill gets frazzled if there are more than three or four buttons on a webpage.}}$
5. $\color{yellow}{\text{Samuel is an eighth grader.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{He wants to prove to his concerned parents the safety of a route he walks with his friends.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Samuel needs to be able to tell a narrative and clearly contrast safety of roads with one another.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Samuel is very technical and loves being able to get into the weeds of a software tool if possible.}}$
6. $\color{yellow}{\text{Elon Musk is an influential businessman who likes to post outrageous graphics (often without fact-checking)}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Elon Musk wants a tool that can show frequencies of collisions between teslas vs. other cars.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Elon wants a quick visualisation tool that he can play with and come up with many iterations using Grok to find a graph that fits his narrative.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Elon Musk is familiar with data science and stats but doesn’t have the time or patience to work through the code himself. He wants a fun UI he can play with in his free scrolling time.}}$
7. $\color{yellow}{\text{Soloman is a design and safety engineer at Tesla.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Soloman wants to better design autonomous driving for Tesla cars to decrease collisions during extreme weather events.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{He wants to use the tool to see under what weather conditions collisions typically occur in Seattle to better design safe and defensive driving for autonomous driving in harsh weather conditions. }}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Though Soloman is technically skilled, he does not have time to search for traffic data, weather data, and analyze it.}}$
8. $\color{yellow}{\text{Little Insurance works at Big Insurance which is an auto-insurance company.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Little Insurance wants to present a narrative for narrowing auto-insurance by neighbourhood to present to Big Insurance in a few days.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Little Insurance wants a tool to quickly quantify and estimate collision likelihood and costs by neighbourhood.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Little Insurance specialises in accounting and cost estimation but not probability or spatial distribution.}}$
9. $\color{yellow}{\text{Pat is a distribution center manager.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Pat wants to minimize the risk to his employer’s vehicles when planning delivery routes.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Pat needs to be able to look between points A and B and highlight particularly dangerous stretches of road, for the times of day that his deliveries go out.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Pat already needs to use many different logistics programs, so he doesn’t want to have to invest heavily in learning this one.}}$
10. $\color{yellow}{\text{Marlon is a taxi dispatch manager.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Marlon wants to establish inclement weather thresholds, past which they will stop deploying taxi drivers.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Marlon needs to be able to look neighborhood by neighborhood, for the weather conditions under which collision risk becomes untenable.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Marlon is highly technically skilled and beyond interacting visually with our tool, wants to be able to download the data themselves for analysis.}}$
11. $\color{yellow}{\text{Shemp is a high school cross country coach.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Shemp wants to find the safest roads to send his runners out on near the school.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Shemp needs to be able to filter the tool by season, to see which roads were safest during the summer and fall, when he is coaching runners.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Shemp is very familiar with statistical tools and wants as many options for data filtering as he can get.}}$
12. $\color{yellow}{\text{Ronnie owns a landscaping company.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{She often has to street-park the company truck in busy areas, and wants to see which streets have more/less collisions involving parked cars.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Ronnie needs to be able to filter only for collisions that involved a parked car.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Ronnie wants to save time – she wants to zoom to the job site on a map, activate her filters, and make a choice.}}$
13. $\color{yellow}{\text{Jim is a newspaper delivery boy. He is assigned two nearby neighborhoods for delivery, but those neighborhoods are separated by a busy thoroughfare.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Jim wants to see which intersection of that road is safest to cross.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Jim needs to be able to filter crashes to just the times of day/days of week that he delivers.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Jim is tech savvy. If we’re not careful, he’ll try to hack the tool himself.}}$
14. $\color{yellow}{\text{Bobby is an urban designer who focuses on visibility for the city of Seattle.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{They want to see the most common places of crash sites in Seattle and see whether visibility was related to the issue.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{They want to be able to cross reference crash data locations with their own dataset of street light locations in Seattle, so they need to see specific site locations of where crashes occurred and when. Ideally, they want a spatial map of crash locations.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{They are trained in spatial analysis tools.}}$
15. $\color{yellow}{\text{dan}}$
$\color{green}{\text{Pam wants to investigate the differences in safety that the new "no turn on red" signs have had on traffic collisions.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{Pam is planning to bring multiple datasets (including the traffic collision one) together for data analysis and would like the data to be clean and easy to understand.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{Maybe Pam works in different coding languages to what are used by the makers of this software.}}$
16. $\color{yellow}{\text{John Pork is a cop.}}$
$\color{green}{\text{John wants to easily identify places with the highest probability of traffic collisions at a given time and day.}}$
$\color{cyan}{\text{John wants a tool that he can easily open up and see what he wants without any input needed (automatically identifying time of day, date, running a model, and showing the output on a map) that he can scroll through easily like google maps.}}$
$\color{magenta}{\text{John does not do tech. John is a little old.}}$
